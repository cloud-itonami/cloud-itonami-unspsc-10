# Business Model: Independent Urban Apiary & Pollinator Services

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-unspsc-10`
- UNSPSC segment: `10` (Live Plant and Animal Material and Accessories and
  Supplies)
- Activity: managed urban apiary operations and pollination-route services
- Social impact: pollinator population support, urban biodiversity, local
  food-production resilience

## Customer

- urban farms and community gardens needing managed pollination
- municipalities running public pollinator-corridor programs
- small orchards and market gardens without in-house apiary expertise
- corporate campuses wanting a verifiable biodiversity program

## Offer

- managed hive placement and colony-health monitoring
- pollination-route service for nearby farms/gardens
- honey/wax harvest coordination (where locally permitted)
- disease/pest early-warning and quarantine escalation (human sign-off,
  never automated)
- colony-health and pollination-impact audit export

## Revenue

- per-hive management fee
- pollination-service contract (seasonal)
- biodiversity-impact reporting package for municipal/corporate ESG
  programs
- honey/wax revenue share where applicable

## Trust Controls

- intervention recommendations require Apiary Operations Governor
  clearance
- any `:high`/`:safety-critical` finding (disease, food-safety-adjacent
  treatment) always escalates to human sign-off
- every monitoring cycle, finding and escalation is logged
- public pollination-impact claims must reference measured sensor data
